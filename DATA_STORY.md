
## Executive Data Story: Performance & Strategic Insights

### 1. Performance vs. Target Analysis

Across H1 2026 (Jan–Jun), 3 out of 6 categories met or exceeded targets, totaling **₹88,282** in cumulative revenue against a benchmark of **₹86,000**.

#### Ahead of Target (+₹9,007 total surplus)
- **Household Essentials**: Total Revenue of **₹21,715** vs. target ₹17,000 (**+₹4,715** / **+27.74%**). Primary volume and revenue driver.
- **Bakery**: Total Revenue of **₹15,410** vs. target ₹12,000 (**+₹3,410** / **+28.42%**). Highest percentage outperformance.
- **Personal Care**: Total Revenue of **₹16,382** vs. target ₹15,500 (**+₹882** / **+5.69%**). Steady performer maintaining positive margin.

#### Behind Target (-₹6,725 total shortfall)
- **Dairy & Eggs** *(Watch)*: Total Revenue of **₹14,090** vs. target ₹16,500 (**-₹2,410** / **-14.61%**). Largest absolute monetary deficit.
- **Fruits & Vegetables** *(Critical)*: Total Revenue of **₹9,790** vs. target ₹12,000 (**-₹2,210** / **-18.42%**). Largest percentage shortfall and lowest overall revenue.
- **Snacks & Beverages** *(Critical)*: Total Revenue of **₹10,895** vs. target ₹13,000 (**-₹2,105** / **-16.19%**). Significant deficit in high-frequency impulse baskets.

---

### 2. Strategic Recommendations

1. **Catalog Expansion & Marketing Allocation (Household Essentials & Bakery):**
   - Reallocate 15% of performance ad spend toward **Household Essentials** and **Bakery**, capitalizing on their +28% target outperformance.
   - Introduce bundle promotions (e.g., breakfast staples pairing Bakery with high-demand Dairy items) to lift basket size and pull underperforming staple categories.

2. **Supplier Audit & Fill-Rate Review (Fruits & Vegetables):**
   - Conduct an operational review of fresh-produce vendor contracts. The **-18.42%** shortfall (₹9,790 vs ₹12,000) stems from stockouts, wastage, and inconsistent daily supply.
   - Restructure procurement terms to improve fulfillment rate and tier fresh SKU pricing to recover lost volume.
